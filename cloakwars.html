The Cloak Wars

Who will you save?

The World

The Pond is a wonderful place to live. The rivers run clear, the markets stay open late, and every evening somebody’s grandmother is handing out something she just baked. Most Nibbles have never left the stretch of water they were born on, and they’ve never needed to.

Life here is good, and it has been good for as long as anyone remembers. But deep in the Mystic Forest, the gnomes hide an ancient secret. Beneath their oldest trees lies The Underbright — a sacred mine containing their most valuable resource: Daylight Crystals. For generations, no Nibble outside the Mystic Forest has known the Crystals exist at all. The gnomes tend The Underbright in silence, and the rest of the Pond has always assumed gnome magic is just how gnomes are.

It was never just how gnomes are.

Every Nibble in the Pond — wolf, bear, raccoon, beaver, ghost, gecko, cock, gnome — was born with a power sleeping inside them. A power that belongs to their species alone, locked away behind a door only a Daylight Crystal can open. The gnomes discovered this a long time ago. They chose to keep it for themselves.

The Acorn Acolytes — 73 gnomes, the priesthood of The Underbright — built their entire civilization on that silence. Forest Cloaks guard the mine. Green Capes patrol the Mystic Forest. Yellow Hoods watch the borders. And at the center of it all, a single Mage Gnome — the only 1:1 in the collection — possesses The Union: the ability to magically wield the power of all eight Nibble species at once.

The Sludge doesn’t bother them. It never has — because it can’t. And they don’t know that.

The Sludge

At the edge of The Outskirts — the rim of the Mystic Forest, famous across the Pond for its glimmercap harvests — there is a hidden glen nobody has any reason to enter. And in that glen, something is growing. It moves when nothing should move. It kills the Land in silence. It has been spreading there for generations, quietly, in the dark. No Nibble in the Pond has ever seen it.

The Wolf Who Listened

The first Red Cloak Wolf wasn’t looking for any of this. He was an emissary — one of the night-running messengers who carried parcels and sealed envelopes between communities across the Pond, trusted by all, welcome everywhere, his red cloak known on every back road. Some nights his route took him along the edge of The Outskirts.

One night, a wheel broke.

The rickshaw tipped, a parcel tumbled off the side, and gravity carried it down a slope into a glen he’d never had reason to notice before. He scrambled after it, and stopped cold at the bottom. In front of him was something he couldn’t name — a slow, black, spreading thing that drank the light. And rolling toward it, just ahead of his reach, were the contents of his broken parcel: crystals he had never seen, crystals he had no idea he was carrying. They landed in the black. And the black recoiled from them like something burned.

In under a minute, he learned three things no wolf was ever supposed to know: that a plague was growing at the edge of the world, that the gnomes were quietly shipping a kind of magic he’d never heard of, and that the magic was the cure.

What matters is what he did next. He didn’t shout. He didn’t storm the mine. He sat with the truth for a long time and understood something terrible: if the Pond found out tomorrow — all 8,545 Nibbles learning at once that a cure existed and the gnomes had been hoarding it — the panic would kill more than the Sludge ever could. Mobs at The Underbright. Crystals scattered. The mine collapsed. The cure lost forever.

So he did the hardest thing. He kept the secret. And then he started building something in the dark. He found eight other wolves. He called it The Order. And when you were invited in — one Nibble at a time, in a whisper, in a room with no windows — you learned two things. First: there is a cure for what’s killing the Pond, and the gnomes have been hiding it from everyone since before you were born. Second: you will carry this truth in silence for the rest of your life, because the Nibbles you love are not ready to hear it.

Two Silences

The Acolytes keep the secret to protect themselves. The Order keeps it to protect everyone else. Same silence. Opposite reasons. Both corrosive. Both necessary.

Today, The Order numbers 270. Nine Red Hood Wolves who started it all. Thirty-one bears in cult cloaks — enforcers who hit hard and ask nothing. Thirty-two ghosts who move through the Pond without a sound, recruiting, scouting, vanishing. And thirty-three of every other species — raccoons, beavers, wolves, geckos, cocks — who were tapped on the shoulder one quiet evening and never saw the world the same way again.

Among them: 33 gnomes in cult cloaks. Defectors. Gnomes who grew up inside The Underbright, who knew the truth their whole lives, and one day looked past the Mystic Forest at the Land that was dying and the neighbors who didn’t know why — and couldn’t breathe anymore. They didn’t leave the Acolytes to tell the truth. They left to carry the same secret under a different flag. One that was actually doing something about it.

The Acolytes hate them most of all. Not because they left. Because every gnome in a cult cloak is living proof that a gnome can choose the Pond over The Underbright. Every defector is a mirror the Acolytes don’t want to look into.

The War Nobody Sees

The Order moves at night. Ghost Cloaks slip through the Mystic Forest without leaving a trace. Bear enforcers move heavy cargo in the dead of night. Daylight Crystals change hands in the dark, and by morning, a hidden glen of the Pond is somehow… less hungry.

The raccoons who live nearby don’t know why. They just know the air smells different.

The Acolytes fight back with quiet eyes. They watch for defectors in their own ranks. They hush neutrals who stumbled into something they shouldn’t have seen. The Mystic Forest has watchers everywhere. And between them, 8,545 Nibbles live their lives. Gathering. Crafting. Trading. Some of them, one quiet evening, will hear a knock at the door. A stranger in a hood will sit at their table, slide something glowing across the wood, and ask them to choose.

Tonight in the Pond

Tonight, somewhere in the Pond, a rogue emissary races against time.

In the Mystic Forest, an obedient Acolyte walks along a fortified wall.

In an empty dimly-lit home, the jewel clasp on a hanging black cloak pulses with a mysterious glow.

The power of a Daylight Crystal will soon be in your hands.Who will you save?
