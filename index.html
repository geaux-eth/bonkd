How to play

Todd wants Grumble's waffles! Drag him left and right to avoid getting BONKD by Grumble's junk. Be careful: 4 bonks and Todd dies.

Catch the tube socks tossed by Booster the Guardian Angel. Each tube sock contains a power-up or a weapon. Weapons have different action buttons that appear on the opposite side of the controls. Use the weapons to help Todd get Grumble's waffles!

From level 3, Grumble's storm cloud throws lightning. Get out of the way, or catch it in the Noggin Bucket and zap it right back at him.

The fine print

Waffles are just for fun. They have no cash value and can't be bought, sold or traded. There are no ads or purchases in this game.

To keep the Leaderboard, the game stores a random player ID on this device along with your board name, picture choice and waffle totals. It does not collect your real name, email, contacts or location. Playing on more than one device? Get a link code under Settings → Profile and type it on the other one. If you clear your browser data without linking, you start over. Your board name is visible to everyone, so don't use your real name.
